# Open questions

Questions that only the owner can answer. When one blocks a slice, add it here and continue with a slice that is not blocked. The owner answers here or in the decision log. Do not delete a question. Change its status.

Status values: open, answered, withdrawn.

## Entry format

```
### Q-<number>: <short title>

- **Status:** open
- **Asked:** <date>, in <phase or file>
- **Question:** the question, in one or two sentences
- **Blocks:** the slice or criterion that waits, or "nothing"
- **Answer:** <filled by the owner, with the date>
```
